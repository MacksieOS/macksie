# Macksie philosophy and foundation names

**Status: current proposed founding philosophy; not an authoritative charter or
adopted Accords.** This naming reference preserves the project owner's wording
provided for the contributor milestone on November 9, 2026. It supplies names,
not authoritative definitions or operating rules. The [draft Accords](../docs/ACCORDS.md)
remain unadopted. Earlier introductory documents leave the authoritative text
pending; this reference does not finalize that text.

## MACKSIE

These are seven single-word values. Preserve this exact wording:

- M — Mutuality
- A — Accountability
- C — Cooperation
- K — Knowledge
- S — Sovereignty
- I — Independence
- E — Equilibrium

## The Ten Foundations

The Ten Foundations remain separate from the seven MACKSIE values:

1. Time
2. Talent
3. Treasure
4. Benevolence
5. Fellowship
6. Round Table
7. Architects
8. Accords
9. Sentinel
10. Looking Glass

Source: the project owner's contributor-milestone instructions and the earlier
Plan discussion, which includes Looking Glass. This list does not assign mandates,
voting rights, titles, pay, or ownership.

## Relationship to the Foundry Glances

The [eight Foundry Glances](README.md) are a separate organizing view: Time,
Architects, Roundtable, Sentinel, Benevolence, Treasure, Talent, and Looking Glass.
The existing Glance spelling `Roundtable` refers to that view; preserve `Round Table`
in the Ten Foundations list. Do not collapse the ten names into the eight Glances or
treat either list as an implemented set of applications.

Use the [Getting Started guide](../GETTING_STARTED.md) to contribute. Definitions,
governance, contributor terms, compensation, and any authoritative charter need
their own sourced proposals and explicit review.
