# ADR NNNN: Short decision title

- Status: Proposed
- Date: YYYY-MM-DD
- Related issue / PR:
- Decision evidence: Pending (link explicit review outcome before marking Accepted)
- Supersedes / superseded by: None

## Context and scope

What concrete need requires a decision? What is excluded? Link source material
and separate known facts from assumptions and unresolved questions.

## Options

Compare the smallest useful option and at least one alternative. Include costs,
operational burden, reversibility, and reasons for the proposed choice.

## Proposed decision

Describe the contract or behavior precisely enough to implement and test. Leave
unapproved details open; recording a proposal does not authorize it.

## Worldwide interoperability, privacy, and sourcing

Cover language/script and text direction, locale versus stored formats, flexible
geography, time zones, portable identifiers and versioned exports. Describe data
minimization, access boundaries, revision/redaction implications, source provenance,
reuse terms, and uncertainty. Mark non-applicable areas with a reason.

## Consequences and verification

What improves, what becomes harder, and how can this be reversed or migrated?
List observable acceptance checks, failure cases, and compatibility fixtures.

## Review record

Link feedback, unresolved objections, and the explicit decision if one occurs.
Possible record states: Proposed, Accepted, Rejected, Superseded. These are document
states, not a new voting process or grant of authority. Keep historical decisions
and link their replacements rather than silently rewriting their rationale.
